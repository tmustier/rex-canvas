Field notes · Week 12

Notes from the tide pools

We walked the north shore at low tide and counted what we could find in the pools below the lighthouse. The water was clear and the wind had dropped by mid-morning.

What we saw

Anemones were open in almost every pool. Two hermit crabs had swapped shells since last week, and a young octopus watched us from under a ledge before it slipped away.
